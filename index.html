ORVIA FOUNDATION · PUBLIC BENEFIT

Capability should create opportunity.

ORVIA Foundation exists to widen access to practical help, learning and opportunity for people who might otherwise be excluded by cost, circumstance or an unconventional path.

WHY FOUNDATION EXISTS

Help should not disappear just because somebody cannot afford the commercial route.

And experience should not be wasted just because it does not fit a conventional CV.

HOW WE HELP

Four clear pillars. Practical outcomes.

Foundation is deliberately narrower than a generic charity or grant-maker. It focuses on where ORVIA can genuinely remove a barrier or create a useful next step.

VETERANS & SERVICE FAMILIES

Turn Covenant commitments into practical action.

ORVIA is veteran-founded, an Armed Forces Covenant signatory and an ERS Bronze Award holder. Foundation should make that mean something: skills translation, mentoring, learning routes, supervised practice and support for Service families.

Veterans are part of the story — not the whole story.

GOVERNANCE

Warm does not mean vague.

Public benefit needs clear decision control, privacy, conflicts management, financial separation and honest legal status.

Human decisionAI may assist administration; people authorise awards.
No charity theatreNo trustees, Gift Aid, charity number or donation claims unless legally established.
Commercial separationFoundation is not measured by sales conversion.
