APPLY FOR SUPPORT

Tell us what you need help accessing.

Start with the human need. Do not send your whole story, evidence bundle, medical records or sensitive documents at this stage.

Foundation support is subject to eligibility, appropriateness, available funding and human review. We do not promise approval times until the process is operationally proven.

What happens next

  1. Initial requestOnly the basics needed to understand the route.
  2. Initial eligibilityWe check whether Foundation is the right route.
  3. Human reviewA person reviews need, proportionality and available support.
  4. DecisionSupport offer or an alternative route where possible.
  5. Delivery + outcomeSupport is delivered and the result is recorded appropriately.
Not for emergencies.

ORVIA Foundation is not an emergency service or crisis line. If someone is in immediate danger, use the appropriate emergency or statutory service.

Human decision.Technology may help with administration, but a person authorises any Foundation support decision.
