ABOUT

Foundation exists to open doors.

Commercial success should create public value. ORVIA Foundation turns part of ORVIA's capability, resources and future success into access, opportunity, practical support and public benefit.

01

The principle

Help should not disappear just because somebody cannot afford the commercial route. Experience should not be wasted just because it does not fit a conventional CV.

02

The four pillars

Access · Opportunity · Learning · Community.

03

Relationship to ORVIA

Foundation can widen access to appropriate ORVIA services, support free learning and create opportunity pathways while remaining separate from conventional selling.

04

Foundation + Academy

Foundation opens the door. Academy builds the capability.
