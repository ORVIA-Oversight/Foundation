02

No guaranteed outcome

Foundation-funded access does not guarantee the result of any meeting, legal matter, evidence review, training outcome or other process.

03

Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, use the appropriate emergency or statutory route rather than waiting for Foundation support.
