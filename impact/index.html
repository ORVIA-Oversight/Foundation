IMPACT

Start honestly. Publish the real numbers.

The Foundation is launching. We will not invent historic beneficiaries, funding totals, stories or outcomes.

01

What we intend to report

As delivery begins, impact reporting should include people supported, supported service places, bursaries, free-resource use, veterans and Service families supported, Story Sessions funded, training places and community partners.

02

How we measure

Every programme should connect input, access, delivery, outcome and learning — not just money spent or page views.

03

Stories with consent

Only genuine approved stories, properly anonymised cases or clearly labelled fictional examples should ever appear here. Receiving help must never depend on publicity.
