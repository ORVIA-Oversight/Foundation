ACCESSIBILITY

The route to help should not be a maze.

Foundation aims for plain English, strong contrast, large tap targets, keyboard access, readable forms and accessible resources.

01

Designed for pressure

People seeking help may be older, disabled, neurodivergent, unfamiliar with complex systems or simply dealing with too much at once.

02

Tell us what is difficult

If a part of the site or application route creates a barrier, contact hello@orvia.org.uk so a human can help find another route.
