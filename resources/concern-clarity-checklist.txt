ORVIA FOUNDATION — CONCERN CLARITY CHECKLIST

1. What is the concern in one sentence?
2. What facts are directly verified?
3. What has somebody reported or alleged?
4. What is interpretation rather than fact?
5. What assumptions are currently being made?
6. What remains unknown?
7. What evidence could reduce the uncertainty?
8. Is anybody at immediate risk right now?
9. Who owns the next action?
10. How will we know whether the action actually helped?

Use this as a thinking aid, not as legal, clinical or safeguarding advice.
