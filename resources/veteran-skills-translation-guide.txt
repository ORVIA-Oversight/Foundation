ORVIA FOUNDATION — VETERAN SKILLS TRANSLATION GUIDE

Instead of only naming a military role, describe the capability behind it.

EXAMPLES
- Planned and delivered activity under time pressure -> operational planning and execution
- Led a small team -> people leadership and accountable supervision
- Managed changing information -> situational awareness and adaptive decision-making
- Worked within clear procedures -> regulated-process discipline
- Coordinated with other units or agencies -> cross-functional collaboration
- Maintained equipment and readiness -> asset, compliance and readiness management
- Debriefed after activity -> structured learning and continuous improvement

Do not exaggerate rank, authority, qualifications or specialist experience. Translate capability accurately and evidence it with examples.
