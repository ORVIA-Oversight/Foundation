COMMUNITY

Practical projects. Measurable outcomes.

Foundation will support work closely aligned to ORVIA's purpose rather than becoming a generic grant-maker.

01

Where we may focus

Potential areas include veterans and Service families, evidence literacy, employability, digital inclusion, life-story preservation and local community capability.

02

Partnership first

We do not need to deliver everything ourselves. Appropriate established charities, community groups, hospices, educational partners and employers may be better placed to deliver some work.

03

No logo theatre

We will not display partner logos without a real relationship and permission, and we will not imply endorsements that do not exist.
