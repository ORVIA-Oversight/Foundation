PARTNERS

Help fund access and opportunity — with a defined purpose.

Corporate sponsorship may be a stronger early route than public donations. Any active sponsorship route must have governance, fulfilment and transparent use of funds behind it.

01

Potential sponsor routes

Subject to approval and funding structure.

  • Sponsor a Story — MIA Story Sessions
  • Sponsor Learning — Academy places
  • Sponsor Access — appropriate Witness Room support
  • Sponsor a Veteran — transition and development
  • Community Partner — a defined programme
02

What partners should expect

A clear purpose, eligibility, funding trail, delivery record and proportionate outcome reporting.

03

What is not available yet

This site does not accept public donations or claim Gift Aid. Financial support routes will only be activated when the legal, accounting and operational controls are ready.
