PRIVACY

Ask only for what is needed.

Foundation applicants may be under pressure or may later need to discuss sensitive circumstances. The initial route is deliberately narrow.

01

Initial contact

Do not send medical records, legal evidence, safeguarding documents, financial statements or other highly sensitive material through the first-contact route.

02

Controlled follow-up

If more information is required, the Foundation team should explain why and move the conversation to an appropriate controlled route.

03

Data use

Information should be used for Foundation assessment, delivery, governance and required administration — not vulnerability-based advertising or retargeting.
